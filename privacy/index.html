Euchre Pad

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

In short
  • Your games, scores and player names stay on your phone, with a backup in your own iCloud. We never receive them.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows an ad after every third game, from Google AdMob. Euchre Pad Plus has no ads.

What stays on your device

Euchre Pad saves the games you score, the player names you type, your settings and your stats in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. If you use iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud account, through Apple, so a new phone or a reinstall gets your games back. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase your games at any time with Clear all games in Past games, which clears the iCloud copy too.

Purchases

Euchre Pad Plus is a one-time purchase made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether Plus is owned.

Ads in the free version

If you have not bought Plus, the app shows one full-screen ad after every third finished game. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect information from your device such as your IP address, device type and settings, app version, and how you interact with the ad. Google never receives your games, scores or player names.

Euchre Pad does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites, so Google does not get your device's advertising identifier from us.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent form appears before any ad, and you can change your choice later with Ad privacy choices in Settings.

Read how Google uses information from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Euchre Pad Plus turns ads off completely.

What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that receive your data.
  • We do not sell or share your information.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

Children

Euchre Pad is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date at the top.
