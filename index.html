Euchre Pad

Keep euchre score the way your table always has: draw the X's, or tap a chip.

Coming soon to the App StoreFor iPhone and iPad

Like on paper

Euchre Pad keeps euchre score the way you would on paper.

Draw the X's

Each X is 5 points. Tap a dot for 1 point, drag straight across dot to dot for 2, or trace a box around an X in one stroke for a loner, 4. Made a mistake? Undo it.

Or tap a chip

Rather tap buttons? Switch to chips and score +1, +2 or +4 a hand.

Your table, your way

  • Play to 5 or 10
  • Green, blue, red or black felt
  • Type each team's names at the top
  • The screen stays on while you play
  • A quick how-to is built in for new players

Crown the winners

Past games shows your last 5 games (Plus keeps every one). Your games are also saved to your iCloud, so a new phone gets them back.
